A Great Day At Home Activity Book: Stem Activity Book

Keep your kid busy for hours with this fun activity book for ages 6-10. Improve math, coding and reading skills with this progressive activity book designed to make learning fun.
This activity book is the first in The Adventures Of Lukey Buke series to concentrate on stem and site word skills. The one side starts with easy word searches to show your child how to complete the task at hand. Then they start getting harder with tips and clues on how to find the words that they are looking for. This side improves many reading and writing skills including picking out site words, handeye and penmanship. On the other side is a series of math games that start easy with examples and get harder as they proceed. There are fun questions, block patterns, handeye games and more designed to improve your childs stem skills.
