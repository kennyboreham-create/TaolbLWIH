Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Learning with Lukey Buke is run by Kenny Boreham in Acton, Ontario. The email list is also called Lukey Learns.

Email

If you join the free maze pack or the digital-product waitlist, we collect the email address you submit, and your first name if you choose to give it, plus any interest groups you tick (Mazes, Stories, Humour, Updates). That information is stored with Mailchimp so we can email you the maze pack and occasional free activities, or a note when digital products are actually for sale. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in those emails.

We do not sell your email address.

Analytics (measurement)

We use simple, anonymous stats to make the games better. A preference bar asks you to Accept or say No thanks. If you choose Accept, we turn on measurement through Google Tag Manager (container GTM-KN5WH259) and Google Analytics so we can see how pages and games are used — for example, when someone finishes the maze-pack form or starts a reading or math tool. If you choose No thanks, or Close, measurement stays off. Your choice is stored on this device.

Advertising cookies

Advertising or marketing pixels stay off. We have not added those pixels yet. The maze-pack form is never blocked behind the preference bar.
