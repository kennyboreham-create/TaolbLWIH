The Vanishing of Nyari

Posted on December 2, 2025 · Category: TAOLB Universe Stories

One moment, Nyari was there — her wide black eyes shimmering with wonder, her fingers trembling as she reached for Lukey’s hand.

The next, she was gone.

No flash. No sound. Just a shimmer in the air and the faint scent of starlight.

Lukey stood frozen beneath the sky of Velari, the word Elyndor still echoing in his chest. The moss beneath his feet pulsed once, then dimmed. The stars above continued their quiet rearrangement, forming a spiral that pointed toward the drifting silhouette of Aetherwyn.

He called out. “Nyari?”

Only the wind answered — a soft breeze that carried whispers he couldn’t understand.

He searched the clouds. The shadows. The glowing moss. But Nyari had vanished completely. Not even a footprint remained.

And yet… something had changed.

The spires of Aetherwyn were brighter now. The city pulsed faintly, like a heartbeat. Lukey felt it — a tug in his chest, a pull toward the sky. Toward the gates.

He didn’t know where Nyari had gone. But he knew one thing:

The gates of Aetherwyn were opening.
