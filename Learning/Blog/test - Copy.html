Dras

Dras Dras is not just fire and flight he is memory, mystery, and misunderstood power. His scales shimmer with deep crimson and ember-gold, like a living sunset. Towering and majestic, Dras commands the sky with wings that stretch wider than treetops, yet he moves with the quiet grace of a creature who has seen centuries pass.

Though feared by many, Dras is not cruel. His voice, when he chooses to speak, is low and resonant like distant thunder softened by wind. He watches the world with eyes that burn not with rage, but with wisdom. Beneath his armored hide lies a heart tempered by solitude and ancient loss.

When he meets Lukey, something shifts. The boy’s courage and curiosity stir something long dormant in Dras a flicker of trust, a chance to be known not as a monster, but as a guardian. Their bond is not forged in battle, but in silence, in shared breath, in the moment when Dras says, “Don’t be afraid.”
