Reading Worksheet

The Picnic Adventure

Part 1 – Read the Story

On Saturday, Leo and his sister Anna packed a basket for a picnic. They put in sandwiches, apples, and juice. At the park, they spread out a blanket under a big oak tree. While they were eating, a squirrel came close and grabbed an apple! Leo and Anna laughed, and then they played soccer until the sun began to set.

Part 2 – Comprehension Questions

1. What day did Leo and Anna go for a picnic?
2. What did they pack in the basket?
3. Where did they sit in the park?
4. What animal took an apple?

Part 3 – Sequencing Activity

Put these events in the correct order (number them 1–4):

___ They played soccer.

___ They packed a basket.

___ A squirrel grabbed an apple.

___ They spread out a blanket.

Part 4 – Vocabulary Challenge

Circle the food words: sandwiches, blanket, apples, soccer, juice, tree

Fill in the blank: Leo and Anna laughed when the ________ grabbed an apple.

Part 5 – Creative Activity

Draw a picture of Leo and Anna at the picnic. Write one sentence about what they are doing.
