Part 1 — Read the story

In the spring, Maya planted a tiny seed in a pot by the window. Every day, she gave it water and a sunny smile. The seed sprouted a small green leaf. Maya whispered, “You can do it!” Weeks later, the plant grew taller than the pot. Maya carried it outside and planted it in the garden. By summer, bright yellow flowers danced in the breeze.
