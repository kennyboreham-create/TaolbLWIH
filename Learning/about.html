How It All Started

I am a father of 3 kids that has a nerdy side. My first 2 kids liked reading so it was not that hard for them to learn. When my 3rd child got to that age she was having trouble focusing and it was difficult for her. I started building an app to help memorize the words she struggled with. After a month of doing the app for 10 minutes a day she started to be able to read the words. Now I want to help make all skills easier to learn.
