How It All Started

How It All Started

I'm Kenny, a dad of three in Acton, Ontario, with a nerdy side. My first two kids liked reading, so learning wasn't that hard for them. When my third child reached that age, she had trouble focusing, and it was difficult for her. I started building a small practice tool for the words she struggled with. After a month of about 10 minutes a day, she began to read those words. I want to make other skills feel a little easier too -- without judgment.
